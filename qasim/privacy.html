Privacy policy — Qasim Mushtarak

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · version 0.1

Qasim Mushtarak is a daily Arabic word game published by Ink Learning Studios.

What we collect

Nothing. This version asks for no account, does not use the internet, and contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (grids played, results, streak) and the vibration setting are stored on your phone only and are never sent anywhere. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes them.

Sharing

"Share result" opens your phone's share menu; you choose where the text (coloured squares, no answers) goes.

Children

The game is aimed at a general audience aged 13 and over and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If a later version adds ads or measurement, this page and its date will be updated before that version is released.

Mise à jour : 15 novembre 2026 · version 0.1

Qasim Mushtarak est un jeu de mots quotidien en arabe édité par Ink Learning Studios.

Rien. Cette version ne demande aucun compte, n'utilise pas Internet et ne contient ni publicité ni outil de mesure ou de suivi tiers.
