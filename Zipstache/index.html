This is Pistache. Ginger kitten, little green spot on his cheek, big fan of walks. You draw the route with one finger.

The rules fit on a napkin:

  • visit every tile once,
  • eat from the bowls in order,
  • finish in his basket.

You'll get it in ten seconds. You'll still be thinking about it at dinner. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and logic always gets you there.

En français : trace la balade de Pistache sur chaque case, mange dans les gamelles dans l'ordre, finis dans son panier. Un petit casse-tête par jour, une seule solution, jamais de hasard.
