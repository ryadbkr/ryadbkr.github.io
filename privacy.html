Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data Collection

Flags Ink uses third-party services (Google Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob) to enhance user experience and serve non-intrusive advertisements.

2. Analytics Data (Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics to gather anonymous gameplay metrics (such as level completion rates and session durations). This helps us improve game balancing and fix software issues.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Google AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier to serve personalized or non-personalized ads according to your privacy consent choices upon app launch.

4. Data Retention & Security

Your local game progress is saved directly on your device via LocalStorage and best-effort anonymous cloud synchronization.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our official Google Play Store listing page.
