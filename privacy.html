Privacy Policy for Flags Ink

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

Inktale Learning ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Flags Ink mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

Flags Ink is designed to be used without requiring any user registration, account creation, or direct input of personal data (such as name, email address, or phone number).

However, the App incorporates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) and services that may collect limited technical data to serve advertisements, analyze app performance, and ensure functional stability.

2. Third-Party Services and Data Collection

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may automatically collect and process certain data, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (e.g., Android Advertising ID), unique device identifiers, and IP address.
  • Approximate Location: Non-precise coarse location data derived from IP addresses to deliver relevant, non-targeted, or contextually tailored ads.
  • App Performance Data: Crash logs, performance diagnostics, and interaction metrics to measure ad efficiency.

Google Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand user engagement and improve game stability. Firebase Analytics collects anonymous metrics, including:

  • Usage Data: App launches, session durations, and in-game achievements.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, and country-level location.

For more information on how Google uses your data, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Children's Privacy

Flags Ink does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The App is targeted at a general audience aged 13 and older. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

4. European Users (GDPR / UMP Consent)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom (UK), AdMob utilizes Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to gather explicit consent regarding cookie usage and advertising personalization. You can update or revoke your privacy choices at any time through the in-app settings.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted on this page.
