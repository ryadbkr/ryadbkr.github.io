About the Game

Flags Ink is a fun and educational mobile game designed to help you learn geography through interactive coloring and quizzes. Fill in flag regions with accuracy, test your speed in Blitz Mode, and build your flag collection!

🎯 Adventure Mode

Color every flag section carefully to unlock new countries and expand your geographical knowledge.

⚡ Blitz Mode

Challenge the clock, guess flags quickly, and push your high score to the limit.

💡 Visual Learning

An intuitive and rewarding way to memorize national colors and flag designs effortlessly.
